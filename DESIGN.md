# Design System: ArMo MS Defender Migration Assistant

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Route You Can See"**

A graphite console for long desk sessions in dim SOC rooms, with one line drawn through it: the route from the Azure portal to the Defender portal, four stops on the way (Prepare, optional Test, Transition, Validate) and a "You are here" marker on the line. Everything else stays quiet so the line can speak. Panels are flat graphite slabs divided by hairline seams; one variable typeface, Mona Sans, is set wide and tight for headings and at normal width for reading; one blue hue marks the way forward (the line travelled, the marker, the one button that does the next step); green, rose, amber and cyan appear only when something has a state. Dark is the default theme, a cool daylight theme is one setting away for managers and approvers, and reports always print on white paper.

Density follows the work. The next-step panel, the welcome hero and the questionnaire (one question per screen) are spacious, because each asks for one decision. Stage lists, the step page and its record panel are dense and scannable, divided by seams instead of nested boxes. Depth comes from tonal steps and borders, never from shadow on a resting surface. Motion is short and has a job: the route draws itself when you arrive, the marker glides when progress changes, a page settles 6px into place, a pressed control gives way to 97%. Nothing loops except the marker's slow pulse and the loader line.

The system refuses the category default of a dashboard of percentage cards under a ribbon. It carries no kicker labels above headings, no glows or gradients except the route halo, no Microsoft logos, and no style set outside the stylesheet or the CSSOM.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One route line as the signature, drawn in SVG on the overview, the welcome page and the intro, vertical below 900px, and straightened into a strip for the questionnaire.
- Graphite tonal layers (ground, panel, raised, well) divided by 1px seams; shadow only under things that float.
- One accent hue (Route Blue), with state colours used strictly for state.
- Mona Sans embedded under the SIL Open Font License, wide stretch for headings, normal width for text; Cascadia Mono only for counts, measurements, timestamps and code.
- 14px panels, 10px and 8px controls, 6px tags, full pills.
- Motion on four duration tokens and two easing curves, with a full reduced-motion path and an in-app Reduce motion setting.
- Every text and background pair pinned to WCAG AA by a unit test, in both themes.

### Departures from the direction contract

The build is the source of truth. Where it differs from `.impeccable/surfaces/src-index-html.md`, the difference is deliberate:

- **Typeface.** The contract named Segoe UI Variable for reading text and mono caps labels with wide tracking for section names and counts. The build embeds Mona Sans for all reading text and uses its width axis for headings. Section names are sentence case in Mona Sans; monospace appears only for counts, measurements, timestamps, key caps and code.
- **No kicker.** The contract placed a "YOUR NEXT STEP" kicker above the next-step title. The build has no label above any heading: "Your next step" is the first item of the context line under the title, followed by the stage, the workstream and the criticality badge.
- **Help links.** Knowledge, Settings and About sit in a compact help row in the sidebar foot, above the offline note, instead of being full navigation entries.
- **Exit demo.** The top bar shows a Demo pill and an outlined "Exit demo" button. "Start my own project" is a separate outlined button in the demo banner.
- **Text tones.** Secondary and muted text (Mist Ink, Slate Ink) are lighter than the contract values; the contract's muted grey would fail the contrast test on the raised surface (about 4.3:1).
- **Accent scope.** The contract reserved the accent for the route travelled, the marker and the primary action. The build keeps the solid fill to those plus identity and progress (see The One Route Rule) and lets the accent's strong and soft tints carry interaction: links, cross-references, inline actions, selected options and the focus ring.
- **Done stops.** A completed stop ring fills with Passed Green, because "done" is a state; the line between stops stays Route Blue.
- **Route draw.** 640ms on the ease-out curve (the contract said 600ms, critically damped). The next-step title is fluid (about 35px at 1440px wide) instead of a fixed 34px.

## Colors

Graphite neutrals, one blue route hue and a five-colour state set. Every colour exists twice, once for the dark default and once for the light theme (the `-light` keys), behind the same CSS custom property, so components never branch on theme. The `system` theme follows `prefers-color-scheme` with the dark values.

### Primary
- **Route Blue** (`route-blue` #7aa2f7, light `route-blue-light` #2f5fd0; `--accent`): the route travelled, the marker and its "You are here" tag, the single filled primary button, the brand mark tile, progress fills (wizard step bars, the loader and splash lines), the active tab underline and the mark inside a chosen answer tile. Also the input caret, checkbox and radio accent, and the border of a selected option or a focused field.
- **Route Blue Strong** (`route-blue-strong` #a4c0fb, light #2450b8; `--accent-strong`, and `--focus` carries the same value): text-weight accent. Links, cross-references, ghost buttons, inline action labels, step numbers, promise icons, and the focus outline in both themes.
- **Route Blue Soft** (`route-blue-soft` #1f2a40, light #e8eefc; `--accent-soft`): the selected state. Selected answer tiles and status options, inline actions, the "Microsoft documentation" chip, step tips, the next-step card at the foot of a step, the welcome Continue card, avatar discs.
- **On Route Blue** (`on-route-blue` #0e1116, light #ffffff; `--accent-text`): text and icons on a Route Blue fill.

### Tertiary (state)
- **Passed Green** (`passed` #9ece6a, light #23704a; `--good`): passed, done, saved, ready, the live offline dot, done stop rings.
- **Failed Rose** (`failed` #f7768e, light #b8283a; `--bad`): failed, critical, not ready, browser save failed, required marks, the danger button.
- **Caution Amber** (`caution` #e0af68, light #915300; `--caution`, with `--warn` as an alias): blocked, not verified, needs review, high criticality, applicability uncertain, sources differ, the deadline icon, "Not saved yet".
- **Info Cyan** (`info` #7dcfff, light #0a6a94; `--info`): the demo pill and banner, in progress, practical-guidance chips, info callouts and toasts.
- **Neutral Steel** (`neutral` #a9b1be, light #4a535e; `--neutral`): not started, medium criticality, undetermined conclusions.
- Each state has a `-soft` tint (`--good-soft`, `--bad-soft`, `--caution-soft`, `--info-soft`, `--neutral-soft`) used as the fill of its pill, badge, callout, banner or conclusion. State text always sits on its own tint.
- **On State Fill** (`--on-fill` #0e1116, light #ffffff): text and icons on a solid state fill (the danger button, done guide dots, ticked loader marks). It follows the active theme, including "Follow my system" on a dark operating system.

### Neutral
- **Graphite Ground** (`ground` #121418, light Cool Paper #f3f5f7; `--bg`): the canvas behind panels, the splash, the intro, the loader veil.
- **Graphite Panel** (`panel` #191c21, light #ffffff; `--surface`): panels, sidebar, top bar, dialogs, answer tiles.
- **Raised Graphite** (`raised` #1f2329, light #f7f8fa; `--surface-2`): inputs, the project chip, hover fills, muted panels, evidence items, toasts.
- **Graphite Well** (`well` #272c33, light #edf0f3; `--surface-3`): the active navigation link, progress tracks, empty wizard bars.
- **Hairline Seam** (`seam` #2a2f37, light #dce2e8; `--border`): every divider and panel edge.
- **Strong Seam** (`seam-strong` #3a414c, light #c5cdd6; `--border-strong`, and `--route-todo` carries the same value): button and input outlines, and the unwalked route (dotted line, empty rings).
- **Console Ink** (`ink` #e6e8eb, light #1b1f24; `--text`), **Mist Ink** (`ink-secondary` #b4bcc8, light #434c57; `--text-2`) and **Slate Ink** (`ink-muted` #8a94a3, light #5c6570; `--muted`): primary text, secondary text and ledes, meta text and captions.
- **Contour** (`contour`, `--contour`): the faint accent lines behind the route, at 6% (dark) and 7% (light) opacity.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`, `--scrim`): the dialog backdrop.

### Fixed surfaces
- **Code Well** (`code-well` #0d0f12) with **Code Ink** (`code-ink` #dfe4ea): code blocks stay dark in both themes.
- **Report paper** (`report-*`): the report is always a white sheet with its own ink, heading, rule, fill and meta greys, three status pairs (passed, caution, failed) and a demo red. It never follows the screen theme.

### Named Rules
**The One Route Rule.** Route Blue is the only accent hue. Its solid fill means "forward" or "chosen": the route travelled, the marker, the one primary button, the brand mark, progress along a path and the mark in a chosen answer. Its strong and soft tints mean "you can act here" or "you chose this". It never marks a status, a category or decoration.

**The State Is Not Decoration Rule.** Green, rose, amber, cyan and steel appear only where something has a status, a criticality, a conclusion or a save state, and always with an icon or a word beside the colour.

**The Pinned Pair Rule.** Every text colour sits on a surface it was tested against. `tests/unit/contrast.test.mjs` reads both themes from `src/styles/app.css` and requires 4.5:1 for ink on ground and on panel; ink-secondary and ink-muted on panel; ink-muted on ground and on raised; route-blue-strong on panel and on route-blue-soft; on-route-blue on route-blue; and each state colour on its own soft tint. A new pairing joins the test before it ships.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Mona Sans (variable, weights 200 to 900, widths 75% to 125%), embedded as two WOFF2 `data:` URLs (Latin and Latin Extended) with `font-display: block`, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the build carries the licence text from `src/fonts/OFL.txt` inside the HTML file and ships it as `dist/licenses/MonaSans-OFL.txt`); fallback Segoe UI Variable Text, Segoe UI, system-ui.
**Body Font:** Mona Sans at normal width.
**Label/Mono Font:** Cascadia Mono (then Cascadia Code, Consolas, Courier New), the Windows system mono; not embedded.

**Character:** One family doing two jobs through its width axis: headings stretch to 104% to 112% with tight negative tracking and read as engineered and confident; body text stays at 100% and reads plainly. Weights sit between the usual stops (560, 620, 630, 640) because the variable font allows it. `font-synthesis: none` and optical sizing are on.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (640, `clamp(2rem, 1.3rem + 2vw, 3.1rem)`, 1.1, 110% stretch): the welcome hero title, at most 20ch. The intro headline and the intro claim use the same voice (claim 700 at 112%).
- **Headline** (640, `clamp(1.75rem, 1.25rem + 1.2vw, 2.3rem)`, 1.1, 110% stretch, -0.025em): every page h1. The next-step panel title uses `clamp(1.7rem, 1.2rem + 1.1vw, 2.2rem)` at 26ch; questionnaire titles `clamp(1.6rem, 1.15rem + 1.3vw, 2.25rem)` at 24ch; step titles 30ch.
- **Figure** (700, 2.9rem, line-height 1, 112% stretch, -0.03em, tabular): the journey percentage on the overview, with the % sign at 1.3rem in Slate Ink.
- **Title** (630, 1.12rem, 1.2, 104% stretch, -0.01em): h2 and panel titles (card titles at 1rem 630).
- **Subtitle** (630, 0.98rem): h3 inside panels and the record.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.55): all reading text; `text-wrap: pretty` on paragraphs, `balance` on headings. Ledes 1.02rem in Mist Ink at 72ch; the step lead 1.08rem at 68ch; objectives 62ch; question detail 60ch.
- **Small** (0.86rem): meta lines, sources, breadcrumbs, hints at 0.8rem.
- **Label** (620, 0.78rem): pills, the demo pill, state pills. Chips 0.76rem 600; badges 0.74rem 640, first letter capitalised. Sidebar section names 0.76rem 600 at 0.01em, sentence case.
- **Route terminus** (600, 11.5px, 0.04em, uppercase): "AZURE PORTAL" and "DEFENDER PORTAL" at the two ends of the route (0.74rem in the vertical route). This is the only uppercase text in the product.
- **Data** (Cascadia Mono, 0.8rem for timestamps, 0.72rem to 0.76rem for counts, tabular): stage percentages and counts in the sidebar, segment counts, workstream counts, stop meta such as "15 of 54", "17 of 99 steps", activity and timeline times, key caps. Code blocks at 0.84rem, line-height 1.5.

### Named Rules
**The Wide Voice Rule.** Width is the display device. Headings stretch (104% to 112%) and tighten (-0.01em to -0.03em); reading text never stretches; reports reset headings to 100%.

**The Mono Means Data Rule.** Monospace marks counts, measurements, timestamps, key caps and code, and nothing else: never names, headings, section labels or sentences.

**The No Kicker Rule.** No small label sits above a heading. Context goes in a line after the title, separated by middle dots.

## Layout

A two-column shell fills the viewport: a 256px sidebar and a fluid workspace (top bar, banners, then a scrolling `main`). Pages centre at 1240px with 28px top, 32px side and 72px bottom padding; narrow pages 880px, the welcome page 1180px, the questionnaire 820px, the new-project wizard 760px.

- **Rhythm.** Siblings in a page are separated by the gap (16px); stacks by 14px; sections by 30px; control groups by 8px; rows by 10px. Panels pad 22px. The compact density setting drops panel padding to 16px and the gap to 12px.
- **Grids.** Overview: the full-width next-step panel, then two columns (1.1fr and 1fr). Step page: guidance plus a 372px record panel that sticks 16px from the top. Reports: three-up cards. Evidence: auto-fill columns of at least 260px. Within panels, lists are divided by seams; panels are never nested in panels.
- **Breakpoints.** 1180px: four-up grids become two-up. 1140px: the step page stacks and the record panel stops sticking. 1000px: the overview grid becomes one column, promises two-up. 900px: the sidebar becomes an off-canvas drawer (300px or 86vw) opened from a menu button; the route turns vertical; every grid, answer grid and form row collapses; breadcrumbs hide; page padding becomes 20px 16px 56px; the intro route becomes a vertical list.
- **Sticky parts.** The form footer sticks to the bottom over a fade into the ground; the step record panel sticks on wide screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth is tonal: ground, then panel, then raised, then well, each edge drawn by a 1px seam. Hover lifts by tone (panel to raised) or by border (seam to strong seam, or to Route Blue on navigation cards), never by a shadow or a lift transform. The route carries the only atmosphere: eight contour lines at 6% to 7% accent and a 15px halo at 14% under the travelled line.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop-over** (`--shadow-pop`; dark `0 18px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, .5), 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, .35)`, light `0 12px 32px rgba(20, 26, 34, .16), 0 2px 6px rgba(20, 26, 34, .08)`): dialogs, toasts, the loader card and the mobile navigation drawer while open. Nothing else.
- **Focus halo** (`0 0 0 3px` Route Blue at 22%): text fields and the search field while focused. A focus signal, not elevation.
- **Flash ring** (`0 0 0 2px` Route Blue, fading over 1.6s): marks the section a link has just scrolled to.
- **Backdrops:** the dialog scrim with a 3px blur; the loader veil (ground at 88%) with a 6px blur. Both become solid under `prefers-reduced-transparency`.

### Named Rules
**The Border Not Shadow Rule.** A resting surface is defined by its tone and its seam. A shadow means "this floats above the page and will go away".

**The One Glow Rule.** The route halo is the only glow and the contour lines the only texture. The fade under the sticky form footer is the only other gradient, and it is functional.

## Shapes

Soft, consistent corners on a small scale, and circles for everything on the route.

- **Panels, 14px** (`rounded.panel`): cards, the next-step panel, the welcome hero, the promises and deadline strips, snippets, the record panel, navigation cards, the empty state.
- **Large controls and insets, 10px** (`rounded.control-lg`): large buttons, callouts, radio cards, answer tiles, the project chip, the brand mark tile, the "why" box, the Continue card, the danger zone.
- **Controls, 8px** (`rounded.control`): buttons, inputs, selects, navigation links, status options, the segmented group, code blocks, evidence items, step tips.
- **Tags, 6px** (`rounded.tag`): chips, badges, segment buttons, inline actions, path segments, the vertical route tag, key caps (5px), the corner of the focus outline.
- **Pills, 999px** (`rounded.pill`): status pills, outlined state pills, the demo pill, answer chips on the answers page, progress tracks.
- **Dialogs, 18px** (`rounded.dialog`): modals and the loader card; toasts 12px; the splash mark 16px; the report sheet 4px.
- **Circles:** route rings, the marker, guide dots, step numbers, status icons, answer marks, avatars, the live dot.
- **Lines:** 1px seams everywhere; 1.5px for numbered circles; 3px route rings; a 2px underline for the active tab. Dotted means "not walked yet" (the route ahead); dashed means "set aside" (skipped stops, the empty state).
- **Icons:** inline SVG strokes in `currentColor` with round caps and joins: 20px at 1.7 stroke, 16px at 1.9, 13px at 2.1, 28px at 1.5.

- **Dialog:** native `<dialog>`, 18px, panel fill, seam border, pop-over shadow, 580px wide (880px wide variant). It materialises over 240ms (8px up, scale 0.98, a 4px blur clearing); the scrim fades in over 200ms.

- **Sequence:** a short, honest list of work that really happened, each line ticking green; about 1.6s, skippable with a button, Escape or Enter, and not shown at all with reduced motion.

### Motion
- **Tokens:** press 100ms (`--t-press`), state 200ms (`--t-state`), enter 360ms (`--t-enter`), hero 640ms (`--t-hero`). Ease-out `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)` (`--ease-out`) for arrivals, state changes and the route; ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)` (`--ease-in-out`) for the two loops and the intro line.
- **Page enter:** only when you navigate to a new page, the page fades in from 6px below over 360ms. Re-renders on the same page do not animate.
- **Small state motion:** colour, border and fill changes over 200ms; disclosure chevrons rotate 90 degrees; a list row's chevron nudges 2px and turns Route Blue Strong on hover.
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every animation completes in 1ms with no delay and one iteration; page, question, dialog and toast entrances become a 160ms linear fade; the marker pulse stops; press scaling is off; the splash minimum and the honest sequences are skipped, and the intro shows everything at once. Under the operating-system preference the marker glide, the drawer slide, the questionnaire strip and the answer tick also stop moving; colour fades still run. The in-app setting (Settings, Motion: "Reduce motion") sets `data-motion="reduced"` on the body, which cuts every animation and every transition to 1ms, stops the pulse, and skips the splash minimum and the sequences.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep one filled primary button per screen, for the action that moves the user forward on the route; everything else is outlined, quiet or ghost.
- **Do** bind every colour to its CSS custom property (`--accent`, `--surface`, `--good-soft` and so on) so the dark default, the light theme and the system option swap without component code.
- **Do** separate surfaces with 1px seams and tonal steps (ground, panel, raised, well), and show hover by tone or border.
- **Do** set headings in Mona Sans at 104% to 112% stretch with negative tracking, and reading text at normal width, 15px, line-height 1.55.
- **Do** pair every state colour with an icon or a word (pill icons, badge dots, ring ticks, dashed rings), and sit state text on its own soft tint.
- **Do** put counts, percentages, measurements, timestamps, key caps and code in Cascadia Mono with tabular numerals.
- **Do** use the four duration tokens and the two easing curves, and play the route draw only when the user arrives on the page.
- **Do** show a loader only after 150ms of real work and keep it at least 400ms once shown.
- **Do** add every new text and background pairing to `tests/unit/contrast.test.mjs` and keep it at 4.5:1 in both themes.
- **Do** set styles from script through `setStyle()` in `src/ui/dom.js` (or `el.style.setProperty`), including custom properties such as `--len` and `--d`; the `style` prop of `h()` and `s()` goes through it.
- **Do** print reports on white paper with the report palette, whatever the screen theme.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put a kicker or eyebrow label above a heading; put context in the line after the title.
- **Don't** use Route Blue for status, categories or decoration, and don't use green, rose, amber, cyan or steel for anything that is not a state.
- **Don't** put shadows on panels, cards, buttons or list rows; shadows belong to dialogs, toasts, the loader card and the open mobile drawer.
- **Don't** add glows or gradients beyond the route halo and the sticky form footer fade.
- **Don't** set names, headings, section labels or sentences in monospace.
- **Don't** uppercase anything except the two route termini.
- **Don't** write style attributes (`style="..."`, `setAttribute('style', ...)`, markup strings with inline styles): the hash-pinned Content-Security-Policy (`style-src` allows only the bundled stylesheet's hash) blocks them and the style silently fails.
- **Don't** load fonts, images or stylesheets from the network: the typeface is embedded as `data:` URLs, `font-src` allows only `data:`, `connect-src` is `'none'`, and the static test rejects `url(http...)` in the stylesheets.
- **Don't** use long dashes (em dash or en dash) in interface text, content or documentation; use a comma, a colon, a full stop or brackets.
- **Don't** show item reference codes in reading text; cross-references show the step title.
- **Don't** use Microsoft logos; Microsoft names are used factually only.

### Accessibility in use
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for every pinned pair, in both themes (The Pinned Pair Rule).
- A visible focus outline everywhere: 2px solid in the focus colour with a 2px offset on `:focus-visible`; fields add the 3px halo; route stops thicken their ring.
- "Skip to content" is the first stop in the tab order.
- On every page change focus moves to the page title (`tabindex="-1"`); re-renders restore focus by `data-focus-key`; dialogs return focus to the control that opened them; the intro traps focus and Escape closes it, as it closes dialogs and the mobile drawer.
- Toasts announce through a polite live region created at start-up; loaders are `role="status"`; bad callouts are `role="alert"`.
- State is never colour alone: icons, words, dots, ticks and dashed rings carry it; guide rows add screen-reader text ("done", "do this now").
- The route is a labelled group of real links; the vertical route is an ordered list; the decorative questionnaire strip is hidden and its text line carries the information.
- Keyboard paths: answer keys 1 to 4 and Enter in the questionnaire; Escape or Enter skips a sequence.
- Preference queries in use: `prefers-reduced-motion`, the in-app Reduce motion setting, `prefers-reduced-transparency` (solid backdrops), `prefers-contrast: more` (strong seams everywhere, outlined pills, chips and badges) and `forced-colors` (route line and marker in `Highlight`, a 2px `ButtonText` border on the primary button).
