# Design System: ArMo MS Defender Migration Assistant

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Noir Champagne"**

The assistant wears the ArMoHub brand, so the app, its reports and the ArMoHub website read as one maker. The ground is Obsidian, near-black with lots of empty space; surfaces are Graphite slabs edged by hairlines; text is Ivory. One champagne accent is a signal, never a fill: the route travelled, the "You are here" marker, the italic accent word in a heading, the gold rule under a page title, the dot beside the active page. Real figures (the journey percentage, counts in the facts line) are set in Signal cyan, and only when they are real. The display voice is Instrument Serif at regular weight, which makes the product read like a firm rather than a dashboard; Inter Tight carries all reading text; JetBrains Mono carries small uppercase labels and data. A light theme (Ivory paper with deep champagne) is one setting away for managers and approvers, and reports always print on white paper with a gold rule.

The route stays the signature: one line from the Azure portal to the Defender portal with four stops (Prepare, optional Test, Transition, Validate). It is now drawn in champagne on black, and the termini are mono labels. The mark is the ArMo puzzle locked inside a safe's dial: a dial ring with sixty minute ticks, a closed piece filled with champagne, and an open piece outlined in the current colour.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Obsidian ground, Graphite surfaces, Ivory ink; contrast pinned to WCAG AA by a unit test in both themes.
- One accent per screen: champagne. Primary buttons are Ivory on Obsidian (Obsidian on Ivory in the light theme), so the accent is never a button fill.
- Thin gold rules instead of boxes: a 44px by 1px rule under every page title, a 1px gold rule under the report cover and above its footer.
- Three embedded typefaces, all SIL Open Font License 1.1, as `data:` URLs: Instrument Serif (regular and italic), Inter Tight (variable), JetBrains Mono (variable).
- 16px panels, 10px and 8px controls, 6px tags, full pills.
- No gradients except the functional fade under the sticky form footer and the gold gradient inside the closed puzzle piece of the mark.
- No stock imagery, no Microsoft logos, no style set outside the stylesheet or the CSSOM.

### Departures from the previous direction

The build replaced the earlier "graphite console with a blue route" direction (Mona Sans, Route Blue) at the owners' request, to match the ArMoHub brand board. Product truth, content, behaviour and layout stayed; colour, type, the mark, the report masthead and the footer changed. `.impeccable/surfaces/src-index-html.md` still describes the earlier direction and is kept only as history; this file is the source of truth.

## Colors

Every colour exists twice, once for the dark default and once for the light theme (the `-light` keys), behind the same CSS custom property, so components never branch on theme. The `system` theme follows `prefers-color-scheme` with the dark values.

### Accent
- **Champagne** (`champagne` #d4b26a, light #9c7a3b; `--accent`): the route travelled and its 14% halo, the marker and its tag, the active-page dot, the active tab underline, progress lines (rail bars, splash line, finale line), the selected-option border, the caret, checkbox and radio accent, the mark in the sidebar and the splash, and the italic accent word in a heading (large text only, 3:1 pinned).
- **Champagne Strong** (`champagne-strong` #e2c78e, light #7a5b20; `--accent-strong`, and `--focus` carries the same value): text-weight accent. Links, cross-references, ghost buttons, inline action labels, the focus outline.
- **Champagne Soft** (`champagne-soft` #1d1a12, light #f0e6d0; `--accent-soft`): the selected state, inline actions, avatar discs.
- **On Champagne** (#07080b; `--accent-text`): text on the marker tag and on the milestone celebration mark.
- **Gold Rule** (`gold-rule` #d4b26a, light #b8954f; `--rule`): the hairline under page titles.

### Figures
- **Signal** (`signal` #47d7ff, light #086c8b; `--signal`): real numbers only. The journey percentage, the rail percentage, bold counts in the facts line. Never decoration, never a state.

### Primary action
- **Primary** (`primary` #f2eee6, light #07080b; `--primary`) with **On Primary** (`--primary-text`) and **Primary Hover** (`--primary-hover`): the one filled button per screen. Ivory on black reads as the brand's confident voice and keeps champagne free to be a signal.

### State
- **Passed** (#8fd3a5, light #1f6b45; `--good`), **Failed** (#f0928a, light #a8282f; `--bad`), **Caution** (#f2a65a, light #8a4b00; `--caution`, `--warn`), **Info** (#a3bedf, light #2a5a86; `--info`), **Neutral** (#aba59a, light #4d4a43; `--neutral`). Each has a `-soft` tint used as the fill of its pill, badge, callout or banner; state text always sits on its own tint. Done route stops fill with Passed, because done is a state.
- **On Fill** (`--on-fill`): text on a solid state fill (the danger button, ticked marks).

### Neutral
- **Obsidian** (`obsidian` #07080b, light Ivory #f2eee6; `--bg`): the canvas, the splash, the intro.
- **Graphite** (`graphite` #111318, light #fbf9f4; `--surface`): panels, sidebar, top bar, dialogs.
- **Graphite Raised** (#171a20, light #f5f1e9; `--surface-2`) and **Graphite Well** (#1f2229, light #ebe5d9; `--surface-3`): inputs, hover fills, the active link, progress tracks.
- **Seam** (#22252c, light #e0d8c8; `--border`) and **Strong Seam** (#353944, light #c8bca6; `--border-strong`, `--route-todo`): dividers, outlines, the route ahead.
- **Ivory Ink** (#f2eee6, light #07080b; `--text`), **Secondary** (#c9c3b7, light #3b3830; `--text-2`), **Muted** (#958f83, light #5f594d; `--muted`).
- **Contour** (`--contour`) behind the route and **Scrim** (`--scrim`) behind dialogs.

### Fixed surfaces
- **Code well** #07080b with ink #e6e1d7 and a 1px seam, in both themes; selection in champagne at 35%.
- **Report paper** (`report-*`): white sheet, warm ink, a gold rule (#b8954f), deep champagne links and italic (#7a5b20), warm rules and fills, three status pairs and a demo red. It never follows the screen theme.

### Named Rules
**The Champagne Is A Signal Rule.** Champagne marks where you are and what moved: the route travelled, the marker, the active page, the rule under a title, one accent word. It is never a button fill, a card background, a category colour or decoration, and a screen carries one accent only.

**The Real Numbers Rule.** Signal cyan is for figures computed from the project (a percentage, a count). A number that is not real is not shown.

**The State Is Not Decoration Rule.** Green, rose, amber, blue-grey and stone appear only where something has a status, a criticality, a conclusion or a save state, and always with an icon or a word beside the colour.

**The Pinned Pair Rule.** `tests/unit/contrast.test.mjs` reads both themes from `src/styles/app.css` and requires 4.5:1 for ink, secondary and muted text on their surfaces, champagne strong on panel and soft, on-champagne on champagne, each state on its own tint, Signal on ground, panel and raised, and the primary button text; and 3:1 for champagne used as large display text on ground and panel. A new pairing joins the test before it ships.

## Typography

**Display:** Instrument Serif, regular and italic, weight 400 only, embedded as four WOFF2 `data:` URLs (Latin and Latin Extended for each style). Fallback Iowan Old Style, Georgia, serif.
**Text:** Inter Tight, variable 100 to 900, two WOFF2 `data:` URLs. Fallback Segoe UI Variable Text, Segoe UI, system-ui.
**Labels and data:** JetBrains Mono, variable 100 to 800, two WOFF2 `data:` URLs. Fallback Cascadia Mono, Consolas.

All three use `font-display: block`, carry the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the build embeds the three licence texts in the HTML file and ships them as `dist/licenses/InstrumentSerif-OFL.txt`, `InterTight-OFL.txt` and `JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt`), and are the only font sources (a unit test checks it). `font-synthesis: none` keeps the browser from faking a bold serif.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Instrument Serif 400, `clamp(2.4rem, 1.5rem + 2.2vw, 3.5rem)`, 1.02, at most 19ch): the welcome hero, with its last clause as the italic champagne accent ("one clear step at a time."). The intro claim uses the same voice.
- **Headline** (400, `clamp(2.15rem, 1.5rem + 1.7vw, 3.05rem)`, 1.04, -0.012em): every page h1, followed by the gold rule. Next-step title `clamp(1.7rem, 1.2rem + 1.1vw, 2.2rem)` at 26ch; questionnaire titles `clamp(1.95rem, 1.35rem + 1.6vw, 2.8rem)` at 22ch.
- **Figure** (Instrument Serif 400, 3.4rem, Signal, tabular lining numerals): the journey percentage; the rail percentage at 2.4rem.
- **Title** (Instrument Serif 400, 1.5rem): h2; card titles 1.4rem; empty-state titles 1.35rem.
- **Subtitle** (Inter Tight 620, 0.98rem): h3.
- **Body** (Inter Tight 400, 15px, 1.55); ledes 1.02rem in Secondary at 72ch; small 0.86rem.
- **Label** (JetBrains Mono 500, 0.58rem to 0.68rem, 0.1em to 0.18em tracking, uppercase): sidebar section names, the product line under the wordmark, table heads, definition terms, the demo pill, the route termini and the marker tag. Never above a heading.
- **Data** (JetBrains Mono, tabular): counts, percentages in the sidebar, timestamps, key caps; code blocks at 0.84rem.

### Named Rules
**The Serif Speaks Rule.** Headings and figures use the serif at regular weight; emphasis comes from size and the italic accent word, never from a bold serif.

**The Mono Labels Rule.** Monospace marks labels and data, small and uppercase, and nothing a person reads as a sentence.

**The No Kicker Rule.** No label sits above a heading. Context goes in a line after the title, separated by middle dots.

## Layout

A two-column shell fills the viewport: a 256px sidebar and a fluid workspace (top bar, banners, then a scrolling `main`). Pages centre at 1240px with 28px top, 32px side and 72px bottom padding; the welcome page 1180px, the questionnaire 820px, the wizard 760px. Siblings are separated by 16px, stacks by 14px, sections by 30px; panels pad 22px (16px in compact density). Below 900px the sidebar becomes a drawer, the route turns vertical and every grid collapses to one column.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth is tonal (Obsidian, Graphite, Raised, Well) with 1px seams. A shadow (`--shadow-pop`) appears only under what floats: dialogs, toasts, the loader card, the celebration card and the open mobile drawer. The route halo (champagne at 14%) and the contour lines are the only atmosphere.

## Shapes

- **Panels 16px**, **large controls and callouts 10px**, **controls 8px**, **tags 6px**, **pills 999px**, **dialogs 18px**, toasts 12px, the report sheet 4px.
- Circles for everything on the route. 1px seams everywhere; dotted means "not walked yet", dashed means "set aside".
- Icons: inline SVG strokes in `currentColor` with round caps and joins.

## Components

### The mark and the wordmark
- `src/ui/logo.js` builds the mark as SVG through the DOM (no markup strings): a 30.4 radius dial ring, sixty ticks (every fifth one long), an inner ring at 50%, the closed puzzle piece filled with a champagne gradient (#f6e7bf, #d4b26a, #a9843f) and the open piece outlined at 1.9 in `currentColor`. Each mark gets its own gradient id.
- Sidebar: the 40px mark in champagne, "ArMo" in Instrument Serif at 1.6rem, the product line in mono uppercase. Splash: a 72px mark and the name in the serif.
- Favicon: a simplified mark (dial ring and the two pieces in champagne on an Obsidian tile) as an inline `data:image/svg+xml` link, allowed by `img-src data:`.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Ivory fill and border with Obsidian text (inverted in the light theme), 8px, 0.9rem 600; hover turns pure white (dark) or lifts to #23262e (light). One per screen, for the action that moves you forward.
- **Outlined, quiet, ghost:** Graphite with a strong seam; transparent with a seam; champagne-strong text with a soft fill on hover. **Danger:** Failed fill, only in a destructive confirmation.
- **Press:** scale 0.97 over 100ms. **Focus:** 2px champagne-strong outline at 2px offset.

### Navigation
- Section names in mono uppercase; links in Inter Tight; the active link takes the raised fill, Ivory text, weight 600 and a 4px champagne dot at its left edge.
- Top bar: breadcrumbs, the demo pill (mono), the save status (green saved, amber "Not saved yet", rose "Not saved for N min" after ten minutes), then "Save package".

### Page titles
Every page h1 (page head, stage head, mission head, or the first h1 of a page) carries a 44px by 1px gold rule 14px below it.

### The Route (signature)
- An SVG climb through six points with eight contour lines behind it. The road ahead is Strong Seam, 3px, dotted; the road travelled is champagne, 5px, over a 15px halo at 14%.
- Stops: done (filled Passed with a tick), current (champagne ring), todo (empty ring), skipped (dashed). Names 15px 600 in Inter Tight; meta counts in mono.
- Termini "Azure portal" and "Defender portal" in mono uppercase at 10px. The marker is a champagne core in a slow pulse with a champagne tag in mono ("You are here", "You made it").
- The marker position comes from `routePosition()` in `src/core/readiness.js`: a later approved stage gate puts the earlier stages behind, so a finished journey shows the marker at the Defender end.

### Questionnaire, cards, inputs, pills
- Card titles in the serif; panels Graphite with a seam, 16px corners, no shadow.
- Inputs: raised fill, strong seam, champagne border and a 3px champagne halo at 22% on focus.
- Status pills, criticality badges and chips keep their state tints; table heads and definition terms are mono labels.

### Feedback, save reminders and celebrations
- Toasts sit bottom right in a polite live region. A sticky toast (`.toast-sticky`, `stickyToast()` in `src/ui/dom.js`) stays until acted on; it carries the save reminder when autosave is off ("Your work is not saved", with "Remind me in 15 minutes" and "Save package").
- Celebrations are native dialogs: a 68px state mark (Passed for a mission, champagne with an Obsidian icon for a milestone such as the sign-off), confetti in champagne, Ivory, deep champagne and a little Signal. The finale draws a champagne line from the Azure portal to the Defender portal with a champagne marker travelling it.

### Reports and print
- A white A4 sheet (max 210mm, 18mm 16mm). Masthead: the mark (34px, #a88442), the wordmark "ArMo" in the serif with "MS Defender Migration Assistant" in deep champagne italic, and a mono line "By ArMoHub · armohub.ch". A 1px gold rule closes the cover.
- Headings in Instrument Serif (cover 26pt, sections 16pt over a warm hairline); body Inter Tight 10.5pt; meta terms and table heads in mono uppercase on a warm fill; links deep champagne.
- Footer: a 1px gold rule, the version line, then a mono line "Made by Arno and Moise of ArMoHub." with links to armohub.ch and the ArMoHub LinkedIn page.
- Print: A4, 14mm 12mm 16mm margins; "Page x of y" bottom right and "ArMo MS Defender Migration Assistant · ArMoHub · armohub.ch · not affiliated with Microsoft" bottom left, 8pt warm grey. Demo reports carry the "DEMO, FICTIONAL DATA" watermark.

### Motion
Four duration tokens (press 100ms, state 200ms, enter 360ms, hero 640ms) and two curves (ease-out `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)`, ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)`). The route draws itself when you arrive; the marker glides when progress changes; pages settle 6px into place. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the in-app Reduce motion setting, animations complete at once, the pulse stops, and confetti is not shown.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep one filled primary button per screen, Ivory on Obsidian (Obsidian on Ivory in light).
- **Do** use champagne for one signal per screen: the route, the marker, the active page, the title rule, one italic word.
- **Do** set headings and figures in Instrument Serif at 400, reading text in Inter Tight, labels and data in JetBrains Mono.
- **Do** use thin gold rules and seams instead of boxes, and leave black space around the main idea.
- **Do** show real numbers in Signal, and no number at all when it is not real.
- **Do** bind every colour to its CSS custom property so both themes swap without component code.
- **Do** add every new text and background pairing to `tests/unit/contrast.test.mjs`.
- **Do** set styles from script through `setStyle()` in `src/ui/dom.js` or the `style` prop of `h()`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill buttons, cards or banners with champagne, and don't use it for status or categories.
- **Don't** add gradients beyond the sticky form footer fade and the mark's closed piece.
- **Don't** put a kicker or eyebrow label above a heading.
- **Don't** use stock imagery, Microsoft logos or invented figures.
- **Don't** write style attributes or markup strings: the hash-pinned Content-Security-Policy blocks them.
- **Don't** load fonts, images or stylesheets from the network: `font-src` allows only `data:` and `connect-src` is `'none'`.
- **Don't** use long dashes (em dash or en dash) in interface text, content or documentation.
- **Don't** show item reference codes in reading text.

### Accessibility in use
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for every pinned pair in both themes; champagne as text only at large display sizes.
- A visible 2px focus outline everywhere; "Skip to content" first in the tab order; focus moves to the page title on navigation and returns to the opener after a dialog.
- State is never colour alone; the route is a labelled group of real links; the decorative questionnaire strip is hidden and its text line carries the information.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`, the in-app Reduce motion setting, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` and `forced-colors` are all honoured.
